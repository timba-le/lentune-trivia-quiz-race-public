The rules

Short, fair, and only mildly ruthless.

1

Getting on the grid

  • Every quiz day, play the Stuff AM Quiz — the one and only official track.
  • Find your own team each day. No assigned pit crews, no politics.
  • Max 4 people per team.
  • Gareth and Aidan can never be on the same team. Two ringers in one car is unsporting.
  • One person, one team, one quiz per day. No double-dipping to farm points.

Your daily playing squad is not your work team. The work team is what counts for the monthly trophy.

2

Submitting your lap time

  • Everyone on the playing team submits the team's score under their own name.
  • A screenshot is required. No screenshot, no score.
  • The site has a quick look at your screenshot for the quiz score circle and tells you what it read. It's there to catch honest mistakes, not to stand in your way — if it can't read the image, or the number differs from what you typed, you'll get a heads-up and can still submit.
  • Open 8am–5pm Monday to Thursday, 8am–3pm Friday, closed at weekends and on NZ public holidays (including Canterbury Anniversary Day).
  • The form locks itself at closing time — late is late. Public holidays count as a freebie; no score needed.
3

How your weekly score works

  • Each quiz is out of 15, so a perfect week is 60.
  • Your weekly score is the sum of your best 4 daily scores out of 5.
  • Your lowest day is dropped automatically once all five are in — struck through on the board and left out of the total. No explanation needed.
  • Played 3 days or fewer? Everything you played simply counts. Away all week? No score, no penalty.
  • Straight sums, whole numbers, no decimals — add it up on a whiteboard if you like.
  • Equal weekly totals? Whoever submitted first ranks higher. The clock on the first log of the week is the tiebreaker.
